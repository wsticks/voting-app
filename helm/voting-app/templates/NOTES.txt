Vote page:
  kubectl port-forward svc/vote -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} 8080:80

Result page:
  kubectl port-forward svc/result -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} 8081:80

The database password is the sample one from values.yaml unless you overrode it.
Votes live on an emptyDir, so they go away if the db pod is rescheduled.
